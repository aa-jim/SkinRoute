## Standard Operational Rules for the Event Plan

### 1. Daily Draw Protocol (The 50% Rule)

- **Rule 1.1 — Strictly Maximize 1X Daily Draws:** Never do full-price single draws (100 Diamonds). Always take advantage of the 1X Daily Discount (50 Diamonds) every single day across the entire 31-day window.
- **Rule 1.2 — Reserve Paid Single Draws for the Final Day:** Full-price single draws (100 Diamonds) are strictly prohibited until **Day 31 (Dec 6)**. They serve only as a final precision tool to bridge any remaining single-digit Crest gap to hit the exact target.

### 2. Recharge & Weekly Diamond Pass (WDP) Rules

- **Rule 2.1 — WDP Early-Stacking:** Recharges on non-event supply days must prioritize **Weekly Diamond Passes (WDP)** over raw Diamond top-ups. This yields the highest diamond-to-cost ratio and activates the daily `+20 Diamond` mail reward over the event's lifecycle.
- **Rule 2.2 — Align Recharges with Side-Event Triggers:**
  - **Daily Rebate Alignment (Nov 6 – Nov 12):** Small daily top-ups or pass purchases are scheduled strictly to clear daily rebate task thresholds (e.g., qualifying top-ups on Days 1–3, 5, and 8).
  - **Mega Draw Micro-Tokens (Nov 6):** Purchase cheap event tokens (10 and 49 Diamonds) immediately on Day 1 to gain initial draws at a `~50%+` discount.
  - **Recharge Milestones (Nov 10):** Direct raw diamond recharges (e.g., 110 Diamonds for 130 Diamonds added) are executed only when needed to clear specific recharge milestone milestones without over-purchasing.

### 3. Premium Supply & 10X Draw Execution Rules

- **Rule 3.1 — Anchor 10X Draws to Premium Supply Days:** Do **not** execute 10X draws on standard days. Perform 10X draws on Premium Supply recharge days (Phase 1: Nov 13 | Phase 2: Nov 24) to satisfy the **"Spend 250 Diamonds in Event"** task simultaneously.
- **Rule 3.2 — Capitalize on Half-Price 10X Draw:** The first 10X draw of the event must be executed on Phase 1 Premium Supply Day (Nov 13) at the discounted cost of **500 Diamonds**.
- **Rule 3.3 — Token Flush:** On Premium Supply claim days, immediately consume all claimed event tokens (**18 tokens per phase**) alongside your daily/discounted draws.

### 4. Promo Diamond Conversion Rule (Nov 14)

- **Rule 4.1 — Maximize Promo Diamond Draw Cap:** On the Promo Diamond conversion window (Nov 14), execute 21 draws using accumulated Promo Diamonds (1,680 Promo Diamonds total). Applying the maximum 80 Promo Diamonds per draw reduces the real diamond cost to **20 Blue Diamonds per draw** (spending 420 real Diamonds for 21 draws).

### 1. Handling Owned Skins & Items (Duplicate Conversions)

- **Base Yield Calculation:** Without duplicates, 119 draws yield an average of **~1,487 Crests** (based on a standard baseline average of ~12.5 Crests per draw from raw drops and tier milestones).
- **Duplicate Multiplier Logic:** Every owned skin or item in the prize pool increases your average Crest-per-draw rate via automatic duplicate conversion:
  - **Owned Basic Skins:** +10 Crests per duplicate drop
  - **Owned Elite Skins:** +20 Crests per duplicate drop
  - **Owned Special Skins:** +50 Crests per duplicate drop
  - **Owned Epic Skins:** +80 Crests per duplicate drop
  - **Owned Emotes/Items:** +3 Crests per duplicate drop
- **Dynamic Adjustment Rule:** As the input for owned skins increases, reduce the projected total draw requirement needed to reach 1,600 Crests.

### 2. Handling Non-Zero Initial Diamonds

- **Initial Diamond Offset Rule:** If the user starts with \(D_{initial} > 0\):
  1. Deduct \(D_{initial}\) from the total required raw diamond additions across non-event days.
  2. Maintain all Premium Supply top-up amounts (Nov 13 & Nov 24) to ensure maximum free token generation (36 total tokens).
  3. Reduce real money spent ($) while keeping the daily draw cadence identical.

### 3. Handling Deficits vs. Surpluses on Day 31 (Dec 6)

- **If Crest Count < 1,600 on Day 31:**
  - Execute additional **10X Draws (1,000 Diamonds)** first for efficiency if the gap is large (>80 Crests).
  - Use **1X Full-Price Draws (100 Diamonds)** if the gap is small (<80 Crests) to avoid overshooting and wasting diamonds.
- **If Crest Target (1,600) is reached BEFORE Day 31:**
  - **Immediate Stop Rule:** Instantly halt all further daily draws and recharges. Do not spend remaining daily diamonds.
